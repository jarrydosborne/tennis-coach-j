Lessons

Three ways to train

Privates and groups are built around technique. Tennis fitness is the fast one: you'll sweat, and you'll hit a lot of balls.

Private

One on one · technique

The quickest way to fix a stroke or build a game plan. We start with where you are and go from there.

  • Stroke mechanics, footwork, serve
  • Match strategy and point construction
  • Tournament and college prep
  • Semi-private for 2 available

Group

Small groups · technique

Learn alongside a few other players at your level. Drills, live ball and some friendly competition.

  • Grouped by level, 3 to 6 players
  • Kids, juniors and adult groups
  • Doubles positioning and play
  • Bring friends and start your own group

Tennis Fitness

High tempo · workout + hitting

Part tennis, part conditioning. Footwork ladders, agility and strength work mixed with nonstop feeding and live hitting.

  • Fast paced, you'll get a real workout
  • Tons of balls hit every session
  • Great for players and non-players
  • Private or group

About

I'm Jarryd

I picked up a racquet at 5 and never really put it down. My dad was my coach growing up, and I've always loved sports, fitness and being outside.

I've coached on and off since college, from country clubs in Dallas to Palos Verdes in LA, and now I'm teaching in the Austin area. I like getting people moving, helping them learn the game, and keeping it fun along the way.

Off the court I'm a dad of 3, I play golf, and I run 20 to 25 miles a week.

Learned it from my dad

My dad has been coaching tennis for over 40 years, all ages and all levels, and he's still on court today. A lot of how I teach comes straight from him: solid fundamentals, hard work, and enjoying the game.
